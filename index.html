About Research CV

I am a senior studying Linguistics at the Ohio State University, advised by Prof. Prerna Nadathur and Prof. Micha Elsner.

My research interests are semantics and pragmatics, and computational linguistics. More specifically, I use experimental and computational approaches to explore how linguistic inferences arise, project, and vary with contexts, and conduct linguistically grounded evaluations of Large Language Models (LLMs).
