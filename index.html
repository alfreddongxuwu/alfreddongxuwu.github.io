About Research CV

Hi! I'm Dongxu, a senior studying Linguistics at the Ohio State University, advised by Prof. Prerna Nadathur and Prof. Micha Elsner.

My research interests are semantics, pragmatics, and computational linguistics. More specifically, I use experimental and computational approaches to explore how linguistic inferences arise, project, and vary with contexts, and conduct linguistically grounded evaluations of large language models (LLMs).

Outside of research, I enjoy classical music, jazz, and photography, and I love matcha, boba milk tea, and coffee 😋
