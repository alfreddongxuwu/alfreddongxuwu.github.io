About Research CV

Hi! I am a senior studying Linguistics at the Ohio State University, advised by Prof. Prerna Nadathur and Prof. Micha Elsner. I am currently applying for PhD programs in linguistics that begin in the fall of 2027. Thanks for stopping by and welcome!

My research interests are semantics and pragmatics, and computational linguistics. More specifically, I use experimental and computational approaches to explore how linguistic inferences arise, project, and vary with contexts, and conduct linguistic and linguistically inspired evaluation of Large Language Models (LLMs).

These interests manifest in my undergraduate thesis, where a large-scale human experiment and a causally inspired Rational Speech Act model combine to provide new insights into the inferential behavior of implicative verbs, as well as in a paper currently under review at ACL Rolling Review (ARR), in which we evaluate how LLMs handle lexical knowledge conflicts in translation by conducting carefully controlled experiments manipulating factors including word frequency, semantic context, and pragmatic framing.

I look forward to further exploring how meaning is encoded in both human communication and LLM representations.
